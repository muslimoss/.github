## About

MuslimOSS is a non-profit, community-governed open-source organization building software, libraries, APIs, and datasets that serve Muslims — and the mosques, schools, families, and developers around them. Our work is guided by the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah according to mainstream Sunni scholarship. We are not a religious authority: where a project requires a religious ruling, we work with qualified scholars rather than deciding internally.

We hold engineering to the same bar as any world-class open-source foundation — versioned releases, tested code, honest documentation, and secure defaults — because Ihsan applies to how software is built, not just what it's built for.

## What We Build

| Category | Examples |

## Get Involved

- **Contribute code or docs** — see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/muslimoss/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for setup, coding standards, and the PR process.
- **Report a bug or request a feature** — open an issue on the relevant repository.
- **Ask a question or propose an idea** — use [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/muslimoss/discussions) rather than opening an issue.
- **Report a security issue** — do **not** open a public issue. See [`SECURITY.md`](https://github.com/muslimoss/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Participation is open to everyone, regardless of background or belief. Respect for the project's Islamic identity and our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/muslimoss/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) is expected of all contributors.

## Governance

MuslimOSS is community-governed, not company-owned. Decision-making structure, maintainer roles, and our relationship with scholarly advisors are documented in [`governance`](https://github.com/muslimoss/governance).
